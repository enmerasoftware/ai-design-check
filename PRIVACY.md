# Privacy

AI Design Check runs on your computer. It has no account, no server of ours and no telemetry: nothing it reads or finds is sent to
us or to anyone else.

- **The checker and `--fix`** read and change files in the project you run them in, on your machine.
- **`scan`, `narrow` and `story`** open the pages you name in your own Chrome, and `story` fetches a URL you give it. Those requests go
  to those sites only.
- **`redesign` and the skills** work through the coding agent you already use. What that agent sends to its provider is covered by
  your agreement with that provider.
- **Installing** through `npx` downloads the package from npm, under npm's own terms.

Questions: https://github.com/enmerasoftware/ai-design-check/issues
