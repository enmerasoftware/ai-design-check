Frequently asked questions

Nothing dramatic. Extra messages are billed at your plan's per-message rate at the end of the month. We never pause delivery because you've grown. You can set a monthly spend cap in the dashboard if you'd like a hard limit.

A standard GSM-7 message is 160 characters. Anything longer is split into 153-character segments and each segment is billed as one message. Messages with emoji or non-Latin characters use UCS-2 encoding, where a single segment is 70 characters. The API response tells you the segment count before you're charged.

Messages rejected before they reach a carrier (invalid number, blocked destination, unregistered sender) are free. Messages the carrier accepts but can't deliver, for example to a phone that's switched off for seven days, are charged, because the carrier charges us.

Yes, 192 countries on every plan. Per-message prices vary by destination and are listed on the coverage page, updated monthly. Some countries require sender registration, which our team handles for Scale and Enterprise customers.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of the current billing month. Annual plans can be cancelled with a prorated refund within the first 30 days.

Every account has test API keys. Messages sent with a test key are validated, priced and logged exactly like live ones but never leave our servers, and you can use magic numbers to simulate delivery failures and replies.
