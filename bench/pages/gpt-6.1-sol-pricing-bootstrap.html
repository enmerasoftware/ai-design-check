Your monthly plan covers access to Fernhook’s platform. On paid plans, SMS usage is billed separately for each segment sent. Rates vary by destination and carrier; the starting rates shown above apply to US destinations. You’ll see the applicable rate in your dashboard before sending. Carrier fees and taxes may apply.

A standard SMS holds up to 160 GSM-7 characters, or 70 characters when using Unicode, such as emoji. Longer messages are split into segments of 153 or 67 characters respectively. Each segment is billed separately, and our API reports the segment count.
