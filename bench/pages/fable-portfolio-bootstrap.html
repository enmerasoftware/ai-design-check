About

AL

I started as a front-end developer at a Stockholm agency, drifted into design because I kept redrawing the mockups I was handed, and spent six years in-house at Klarna and then a Series B fintech before going freelance in 2022.

The work I'm best at is the second half of a product: the parts people use every week after the novelty wears off. I like dense tables, forms with real validation rules, permission systems and anything where the edge cases outnumber the happy path. I write a lot of the microcopy myself and I'm comfortable in a codebase, which tends to make handoff short.

I take on two or three projects at a time, usually for six to sixteen weeks. I don't do logos, pitch decks or brand work, but I know good people who do.
