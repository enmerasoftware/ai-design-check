How does usage-based pricing work?+

You only pay for the messages you send, in addition to any monthly plan fee. Message rates vary by destination and volume; we'll always make your rates clear before you send.

Can I change plans later?+

Absolutely. You can move to a different plan as your needs change. Get in touch and we'll help you find the right fit.

Are there any setup fees or contracts?+

There are no setup fees or long-term contracts for Seedling or Grove. You can cancel your monthly plan at any time.

What kind of support do you offer?+

Every plan includes access to our documentation and community. Grove includes priority email support, while Forest comes with dedicated help.
