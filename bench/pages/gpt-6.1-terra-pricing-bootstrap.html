Good to know

Questions?
We’ve got answers.

Still have something on your mind? Get in touch and we’ll be happy to help.

How does usage-based pricing work?

You pay for the messages you send, based on your plan’s message rate and destination. Your dashboard shows your usage so you can keep track as you go.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can move to a different plan as your messaging needs change. Get in touch with our team and we’ll help you make the switch.

Are there any setup fees or contracts?

There are no setup fees for Starter or Growth, and you can pay month to month. Custom Scale agreements are tailored to your needs.

Do message rates vary by country?

Yes. Rates depend on the destination and carrier. The starting rates shown above are for US SMS; contact us for rates in other countries.

What happens if I need help integrating?

Our documentation and community are available to every team. Growth customers also receive priority email support, while Scale includes dedicated onboarding.
