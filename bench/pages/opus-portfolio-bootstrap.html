About

I'm Ada. I've spent eleven years designing products where getting it wrong costs someone time, money or their health, so I care a lot about the boring details.

I studied interaction design at Umeå Institute of Design, then worked at Spotify and as design lead at a Stockholm fintech before going freelance. I usually join a team for three to six months, run research myself, and hand over Figma files and documentation that engineers actually enjoy using.
