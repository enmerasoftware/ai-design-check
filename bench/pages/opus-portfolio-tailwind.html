About

I start every project by sitting with the people who'll use the thing: nurses on night shift, traders before market open, parents at the kitchen table. Most good design decisions come from there.

Before going independent in 2021 I led design at Klarna's merchant tools team and worked at an agency in Copenhagen. I work alone or embedded in your team, usually for three to six months, and I'm comfortable owning research, interaction design and visual design end to end.
