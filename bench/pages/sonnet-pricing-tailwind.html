Frequently asked questions

What counts as one message?

One segment of up to 160 GSM characters, or 70 if the text contains emoji or non-Latin characters. Longer texts are split into segments and billed per segment.

How is international pricing handled?

Rates depend on the destination country and are listed in the dashboard. You will always see the price of a message in the API response before you send at scale.

What happens if I go over my included messages?

Nothing breaks. Extra messages are billed at your plan's per-message rate at the end of the month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply from your next billing date and unused included messages do not roll over.

Do you charge for failed messages?

No. If a carrier rejects a message, it is not billed.
