Everything you need to get paid for the work you actually do

Most time trackers were built for agencies with managers. Tallyhoo was built for one person wearing every hat.

Automatic app detection

Tallyhoo notices when you open a client's Figma file or jump on a Zoom call and starts the right timer for you. Forgot to stop it? It asks before counting idle time.

One-click invoices

Pick a client and a date range. Tallyhoo groups the hours by task, applies your rate and sends a branded PDF with a Stripe payment link.

Honest profitability reports

See your real hourly rate per client once revisions and unbilled calls are counted. You'll know which retainers to renegotiate before the next one lands.

Up and running before your coffee cools

  1. Step 1

    Add your clients and rates

    Import from your invoicing tool or type them in. Hourly, day rate or fixed fee per project, all supported.

  2. Step 2

    Work like you normally do

    The menu bar app does the tracking. You can also tap a timer on your phone when you're sketching away from the desk.

  3. Step 3

    Review Friday, invoice Monday

    A weekly digest shows what was tracked. Fix anything that looks off, then send the invoice from the same screen.
