Real bread, baked before you're awake.

We're a small bakery run by four people who think a good loaf needs only flour, water, salt and a lot of patience. The ovens go on at 4am. By seven the shop smells like it should, and by lunchtime the sourdough is usually gone.

What's on the counter

The breads are daily. Pastries change with what the market has. Cakes are made to order, two days' notice.

Country sourdough

36-hour ferment, stoneground white and a little rye. Thick crust, open crumb. Our bread for everything. £4.80

Seeded wholemeal tin

Soft, square and sensible. Sunflower, linseed and pumpkin seeds. Toasts beautifully, lasts four days. £4.20

Butter croissants

Laminated the evening before with Normandy butter. Plain, almond or pain au chocolat. Out of the oven at 7:15 and 9:30. from £3.20

Cardamom buns

Swedish-style, twisted by hand, finished with pearl sugar. The thing people queue for on Saturdays. £3.60

Focaccia

Dimpled, olive-oil-soaked and topped with whatever's good: rosemary and sea salt most days, roasted tomatoes in summer. £3.00 a slab

Celebration cakes

Lemon drizzle, Victoria sponge, dark chocolate and olive oil. Six to twelve inches, written on if you like. from £28

Everything is baked in a kitchen that handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and sesame. Ask us about ingredients, we're happy to check.
