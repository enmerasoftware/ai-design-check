A little about me

01 / HUMAN FIRST
Curious by nature.
Designer by practice.

Good design starts with listening.
I bring curiosity, clarity, and a little Scandinavian simplicity to every project.

I’m a freelance product designer based in Stockholm, working with people everywhere. Over the past 8 years, I’ve helped startups and growing teams turn big ideas into thoughtful digital experiences.

From the first messy sketch to the final tiny interaction, I care about the details that make a product feel right. Away from my desk, you’ll find me by the water, in a bookshop, or perfecting a very slow morning coffee.

Selected work · 2025

Folio — Finance, with clarity.

I partnered with Folio to rethink how independent professionals understand their finances. We brought balances, cash flow, and transactions into one clear overview, with a visual hierarchy that helps people find what matters.

My role: Product strategy, experience design, interface design, and a reusable component system. Built in close collaboration with the founding team and engineers.

Selected work · 2024

Still — A little room to breathe.

Still makes it easier to find a moment of calm in a busy day. I shaped the brand direction and mobile experience around gentle guidance, simple choices, and an uncluttered listening experience.

My role: Brand direction, user journeys, mobile interface design, and interaction prototypes. A calm visual language carries through from the first welcome to the final breath.
