Can I try Fernhook before paying?+

Absolutely. Every Seed account comes with 100 free SMS credits and full API access. Use the sandbox to test your integration, then send to verified numbers. No credit card needed.

How do SMS credits work?+

One credit covers one standard SMS segment to a US number. Longer messages, Unicode characters, and international destinations may use more credits. You can review the cost before sending in your dashboard. Monthly credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over.

What happens if I go over my plan?+

On Grow, additional messages are billed at your destination’s usage rate, starting at $0.006 per US SMS segment. Set a spending limit and usage alerts in your dashboard to stay in control. Seed sending pauses when your free credits run out.

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?+

Yes. Upgrade whenever you need more capacity. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your billing term, and your API integration stays in place. Annual plans are billed upfront and remain active through the paid term.

Do you support international messaging?+

Yes, Fernhook reaches more than 190 countries. Availability, sender registration, and pricing depend on the destination. Contact our team for rates and help getting your sender approved.
