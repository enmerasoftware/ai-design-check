About

I trained as an industrial designer in Gothenburg, then moved into interface work when I realised most of the problems I cared about were on screens. I now lead design for small product teams and work as a freelancer on projects of six to twelve weeks.

I focus on research-led flows, careful content design and systems that a small team can maintain. I pair well with engineers and I write specs people actually read.
