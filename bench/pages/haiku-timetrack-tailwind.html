Questions

Does Tallyhoo work offline? +

Yes. Timers keep running without a connection and sync as soon as you are back online.

Can I import hours from another tool? +

You can import CSV files from Toggl, Harvest and Clockify. Projects and clients are matched by name.

Do my clients need an account? +

No. Clients receive invoices and optional read-only hour summaries by email.

What happens after the trial? +

You choose a plan. If you do nothing, your data stays in read-only mode for 30 days.
