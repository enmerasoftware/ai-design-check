Frequently asked questions

What counts as one message?+

One segment: up to 160 GSM-7 characters, or 70 characters if your text includes emoji or other Unicode. Longer messages are split and billed per segment. The API response tells you how many segments were used.

Do I pay for messages that fail?+

No. If a carrier rejects a message or the number is invalid, we refund it automatically. You only pay for messages the carrier accepts.

What is 10DLC and do I need it?+

US carriers require businesses sending from local numbers to register a brand and campaign. On Growth and Scale we file the registration for you; carrier fees of about $4 one-time and $2–$10 per month pass through at cost.

Can I switch plans later?+

Any time from the dashboard. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

Is there a free trial?+

Starter has no monthly fee and includes $2 of credit so you can test. Growth comes with a 14-day trial; you will not be charged until it ends.

How do volume discounts work on Scale?+

Rates step down automatically past 1 million and 5 million messages a month. For committed volume above that, we will quote a custom rate.
