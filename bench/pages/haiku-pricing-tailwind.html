Frequently asked questions

Do unused messages roll over? +

No. Your monthly allowance resets on the billing date. Messages beyond the allowance are billed at the plan's overage rate.

Am I charged for failed deliveries? +

Messages that the carrier rejects before delivery are not billed. You can see the status of every message in the dashboard and in webhook events.

Can I switch plans mid-month? +

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the next billing date.

Do you support sender ID registration? +

We handle registration for countries that require it, such as the UK and India. Registration typically takes 3 to 10 business days.

Is there a free sandbox? +

Yes. The sandbox accepts test numbers and returns realistic delivery events, with no charges and no sign-up limits.
