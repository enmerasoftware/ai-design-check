Good questions.

Does Brightmoss send replies automatically?+

You’re always in control. Brightmoss suggests a draft for your team to review and edit. A person decides what gets sent, every time.

Where does Brightmoss get its answers?+

From the help articles, policies, and knowledge you connect. Drafts include references so your team can check the source before sending.

Is Brightmoss right for a small team?+

Yes. Whether you’re the whole support department or part of a growing team, Brightmoss helps you get from a customer question to a thoughtful first draft.
